About

Electronic engineer specialised in embedded systems, completing a PhD in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the University of Cagliari, with a research stay in the PULP Group at ETH Zürich. My work covers the whole development flow of low-power digital hardware for AI: neural network training, RTL design in Verilog, implementation on FPGA and ASIC, and deployment of the trained network on the designed system.
